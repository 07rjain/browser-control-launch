# Quiet Routing

A browser starts with scattered intentions: pages, tabs, remembered preferences, and repeated actions. Quiet Routing expresses the instant those separate intentions find a common direction. Its conceptual seed is the rounded turn of a browser workflow, repeated until individual routes become a coherent, breathable field. This is a computational composition, not a random texture behind a headline.

A meticulously crafted algorithm produces families of cubic trajectories. A seeded generator perturbs their spacing, curvature, and entry heights within tight bounds; no strand is drawn by hand. Correlated offsets preserve the family resemblance while local variation breaks mechanical symmetry. Reproducibility matters: the same seed must always return exactly the same geometry.

The empty center is as deliberate as the marks. A shaped exclusion region reserves space for language and action, while the field gathers near the preview below. Deep computational expertise should be visible in restraint: density lives at the edges, crossing is limited, and every curve has room to be read. Rose, mauve, and pale plum connect the drawing to the chosen interface palette.

Time reveals the paths rather than endlessly disturbing them. One brief entry trace resolves into stillness; a visitor selecting a feature can send another finite signal along an existing route. The artwork must respect reduced motion, and its master-level implementation spends no animation frames maintaining a settled composition. The p5 study exposes seed, strand count, spread, and curvature so the relationship between rule and result remains inspectable.

The final system should feel painstakingly refined: clean tangent joins, consistent line weights, subtle asymmetry, and no ornamental excess. Its beauty comes from running a precise process and observing order emerge. The website uses the same generated geometry as a lightweight SVG, while the standalone p5 viewer allows exploration without placing an art editor in the product flow.
